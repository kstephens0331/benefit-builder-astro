## Why Cape Girardeau employers offer Section 125 benefits

Cape Girardeau is the seat of Cape Girardeau County and sits on the Mississippi River in southeast Missouri, where it serves as the commercial and medical hub for the Bootheel and the Ozark foothills. The city pulls workers and patients from a wide swath of Missouri, Illinois, and the surrounding region, and its largest employers reflect that role. Two competing health systems, Saint Francis Healthcare System and SoutheastHEALTH, are among the biggest job providers in the area, each employing thousands. Procter and Gamble runs one of its largest U.S. plants here, a multi-million-square-foot operation producing paper products. Southeast Missouri State University adds a large base of faculty and staff, and a deep bench of retailers, clinics, senior care providers, and small businesses fills out a regional center that serves far more people than the city's own population.

Those employers compete hard for nurses, technicians, line operators, faculty, and skilled trades, and a benefits package is part of how they win and keep that talent. A Section 125 cafeteria plan lets a Cape Girardeau employer offer pre-tax health, dental, vision, and dependent care benefits that genuinely raise the value of a paycheck without raising the wage line. For a hospital staffing multiple shifts, a manufacturer with a large hourly crew, or a university department balancing certified and classified roles, that is a recruiting and retention tool that costs the budget nothing extra to put in place.

## How the plan works here

A Section 125 plan lets an employee make a qualified election, and that amount comes out before federal income tax, before Missouri's flat 4.7 percent state income tax, and before FICA is calculated. The employee keeps more take-home pay on the same gross wage. As a rolled-in fact, because the elected dollars sit outside FICA wages, the employer's matching payroll cost on those dollars is lower too. For Cape Girardeau employers running steady weekly or biweekly payrolls, the benefit shows up on every paycheck once the plan is set up, with no added office work to capture it. The same structure fits employers in neighboring Jackson, Scott City, and Sikeston, where the same healthcare, education, and manufacturing base drives the local economy.
