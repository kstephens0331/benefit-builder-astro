## Why Festus employers offer Section 125 benefits

Festus sits in Jefferson County, Missouri, about 30 miles south of St. Louis along the Interstate 55 corridor. Together with the adjoining Twin City of Crystal City, it anchors the southern edge of the St. Louis metro, close enough to draw on metro labor but with an economy of its own. This stretch of Jefferson County carries a long industrial history. Crystal City grew up around plate-glass manufacturing on the Mississippi River, neighboring Herculaneum was a lead-smelting town for generations, and the broader region still feeds into Missouri's mining and minerals industry to the south. Today the local economy runs on healthcare, the skilled trades, distribution along the I-55 corridor, retail, and a dense base of small businesses. Mercy Hospital Jefferson in Festus is a major regional employer, and a wide range of contractors, clinics, and family-owned firms make up the rest.

These employers compete for nurses, technicians, tradespeople, and counter staff against the pull of the larger St. Louis metro just up the highway, and a benefits package is part of how a smaller employer keeps that competition fair. A Section 125 cafeteria plan lets a Festus employer offer pre-tax health, dental, vision, and dependent care benefits that raise the real value of a paycheck without raising the wage line. For a dental office, a plumbing or electrical contractor, a hospital department, or a family-owned retailer, that is a recruiting and retention tool with a predictable monthly cost.

## How the plan works here

A Section 125 plan lets an employee make a qualified election, and that amount comes out before federal income tax, before Missouri's flat 4.7 percent state income tax, and before FICA is calculated. The worker keeps more take-home pay on the same gross wage. As a rolled-in fact, because the elected dollars sit outside FICA wages, the employer's matching payroll cost on those dollars is lower too. The plan works across pay frequencies and across the full range of Jefferson County industries, and once it is set up the benefit shows up on every paycheck with no added office work. Employers in neighboring Crystal City, Herculaneum, and Arnold use the same structure for the same reasons.
