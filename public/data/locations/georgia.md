## How the plan works for your workforce

A Section 125 plan lets employees elect qualified benefits before payroll taxes and income taxes are calculated, so those dollars come out of gross pay rather than take-home pay. The everyday benefit is straightforward: staff get the coverage they need at a lower out-of-pocket cost, which makes your total compensation more competitive without raising base wages. As a rolled-in matter of fact, because elected dollars leave the taxable wage base, the employer's payroll cost on those dollars is lower too, but the reason employers in Atlanta, Savannah, Augusta, Columbus, Macon, and the surrounding counties adopt these plans is benefit value and retention, not a tax angle.

## What can run pre-tax

A cafeteria plan can run a defined set of qualified benefits through pre-tax payroll: group insurance premiums (medical, dental, vision, and similar coverage), health flexible spending accounts, dependent care FSAs, health savings account contributions made through payroll, and dependent care assistance. Each lowers taxable wages for the elected amount. Some benefits, such as long-term care insurance, generally do not qualify inside a cafeteria plan and are handled separately.
