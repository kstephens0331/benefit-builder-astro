These are employers who compete for nurses, technicians, drivers, instructors, and skilled production workers, and a benefits package is part of how they win those hires and keep them. A Section 125 cafeteria plan lets a Poplar Bluff employer put pre-tax health, dental, vision, and dependent care benefits in front of W-2 staff, which raises the real value of a paycheck without raising the wage line. For a hospital staffing around the clock, a freight operation running steady routes, or a family-owned shop on the square, that is a recruiting and retention tool that fits the budget.

## How the plan works here

A Section 125 plan lets a worker make a qualified election, and that amount comes out before federal income tax, before Missouri's flat 4.7 percent state income tax, and before FICA is calculated. The employee keeps more take-home pay on the same gross wage. As a rolled-in fact, because the elected dollars sit outside FICA wages, the employer's matching payroll cost on those dollars is lower as well. Once the plan is set up, the benefit shows up on every paycheck with no added workload for the payroll team. The same logic carries to nearby employers in Dexter, Doniphan, and Sikeston, where the same regional industries and steady payrolls make a cafeteria plan a straightforward fit.
