## Section 125 cafeteria plans for Southfield employers

Southfield is one of the densest office markets in Michigan. Sitting in southern Oakland County just northwest of Detroit, where the I-696, the Lodge Freeway, and Northwestern Highway converge, the city packs millions of square feet of office space into a compact business district and serves as a regional base for corporate headquarters, professional services firms, and financial and healthcare companies. For that kind of employer -- one that competes for accountants, attorneys, IT staff, and administrators -- a Section 125 cafeteria plan is a straightforward way to make a compensation offer stronger. It lets employees pay for qualified benefits with pre-tax dollars, which stretches their take-home pay, and it gives the employer a recruiting and retention tool that fits a salaried, white-collar workforce.

## Southfield's office economy

The city built its identity around being a business address. Its skyline of office towers along the Northwestern Highway corridor houses national and regional companies, and the surrounding district is thick with law firms, accounting and consulting practices, insurance and financial services offices, engineering and architecture firms, and corporate back-office operations. ## Why the plan fits Southfield's payrolls

Professional and corporate employers in Southfield run predictable pay periods without seasonal swings or irregular hours, so pre-tax benefit deductions stay stable from one cycle to the next and the administration stays simple. That matters for the kind of competition these firms face. A mid-sized accounting practice or law firm in Southfield is recruiting from the same talent pool as much larger Detroit-metro employers, and a [Section 125 plan](/services/employers) lets it put a benefits package in front of a candidate that reads as competitive without the cost of matching a Fortune 500 plan dollar for dollar.

For the employee, the value is concrete: pre-tax dollars set aside for health, dental, and vision premiums, medical and dependent-care expenses, and other qualified benefits mean more usable income on costs they were already paying. For the employer, a workforce that understands the plan enrolls at a higher rate, which is why employee education is part of how Benefit Builder runs it -- not a footnote. We handle the payroll coordination that keeps deductions accurate every cycle and the education that lifts enrollment so the plan delivers its full value across an office roster. ## A note on cost

Because qualified pre-tax elections come out before FICA is calculated, an employer's matching FICA falls on the dollars routed through the plan, which helps offset what it costs to offer. That is a genuine effect, but it is a byproduct. The reason a Southfield employer adopts a cafeteria plan is the benefit itself: a stronger offer for the professionals it is trying to hire and keep.
