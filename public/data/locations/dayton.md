## Why Dayton manufacturers and offices use a plan

A production floor of hourly workers and an office of salaried staff both generate the consistent, predictable payrolls that a cafeteria plan serves well. For a Dayton manufacturer or aerospace supplier trying to hold onto experienced machinists and technicians, or a clinic competing for nurses, the plan lets the benefits portion of an offer go further for each employee without raising the wage line. Under the plan, employees can elect health, dental, and vision premiums, health care and dependent care flexible spending accounts, and health savings account contributions on a pre-tax basis, so they keep more of each paycheck for the same coverage. Because those elections come out before income and payroll taxes are figured, the employer's share of FICA on participating wages is reduced too, a routine result of how the plan works rather than the reason to adopt it.

The execution is the part most employers should not have to manage. A plan only delivers when employees understand it and actually enroll, and when every election is coordinated correctly with each payroll run. Benefit Builder handles both sides: we run the payroll coordination so deductions post accurately every cycle, and we provide the employee education that keeps participation strong.
