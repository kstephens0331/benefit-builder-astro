A Section 125 plan, also called a cafeteria plan, is one of the most practical ways for an employer to strengthen its benefits without adding cost or complexity. This guide walks through what you actually get when you put a plan in place, and what it means for your team and your office.

## A stronger benefits offering

At its core, a Section 125 plan lets your employees pay for qualified benefits with pre-tax dollars through payroll. For your people, that means more value from every paycheck and access to benefits they will actually use. For you, it means a more competitive offer when you are hiring and a real reason for good employees to stay. Because the deductions are pre-tax, your payroll costs come down as well, but the benefit to your team is the point.
