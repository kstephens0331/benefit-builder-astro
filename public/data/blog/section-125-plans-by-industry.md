Section 125 cafeteria plans work across almost every kind of business, but the reason an employer values one tends to differ by field. Here is where a plan fits best, and what it tends to solve in each.

## Manufacturing

Manufacturers compete for skilled labor on tight margins. A plan gives production teams pre-tax benefits that help with recruiting and retention, and it works cleanly with weekly and biweekly payroll. See [Section 125 plans for manufacturers](/industries/manufacturing).

## Senior care and nursing

Turnover is the constant pressure in senior care. A plan gives caregivers a valuable benefit that helps them stay, and enrollment can run across every shift, including nights and weekends. See [Section 125 plans for senior care employers](/industries/senior-care).

## Trades and contractors

A small HVAC, electrical, roofing, or construction shop can offer the kind of benefits people expect from a large employer, with one monthly fee and no setup cost. See [Section 125 plans for trades and contractors](/industries/trades-contractors).

## Schools and education

Schools run on tight budgets and value benefits that help retain staff. A plan works with monthly and semimonthly school pay calendars, and it covers faculty and support staff alike. See [Section 125 plans for schools](/industries/schools-education).

## What stays the same across industries
